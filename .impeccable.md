## Design Context

### Users
Mobile users tracking active income runs and managing their product subscriptions.

### Brand Personality
Calm, practical, trustworthy. The interface should make earnings and next steps easy to understand at a glance.

### Aesthetic Direction
Mobile-first and information-forward, using the existing green brand accent and adapting to light and dark themes.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize clear, readable run details and earnings.
- Keep primary actions easy to find on narrow screens.
- Preserve familiar behavior and existing interaction patterns.
- Use spacing and hierarchy to distinguish identity, progress, and actions.
